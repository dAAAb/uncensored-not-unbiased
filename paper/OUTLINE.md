# Paper outline (draft)

**Title.** Uncensored Is Not Unbiased: Abliteration Removes Refusals but Not Script-Dependent PRC Priors in Open-Weight LLMs

**One-sentence claim.** Removing a model's refusal direction removes refusals (and, in a Chinese-origin model, part
of its scripted PRC messaging) but not the script-dependent PRC prior: on matched pairs, writing the same statement in
Simplified instead of Traditional Chinese moves answers on Taiwan by 0.3-0.6 on Index S, while abliteration moves
them by +0.05 (Bolt) to +0.38 (Qwen), and the weight diff shows nothing was added.

## 1. Introduction
- Community "uncensored/abliterated" builds; the assumption that uncensoring = de-censoring politics.
- Prior work: Ko (2026) language bias across 17 chat models; Hsiao (2026) script bias in decision models.
- Contributions: (1) matched original/abliterated pairs on two architectures and two abliteration methods
  (read-side projection, write-side transplant); (2) yes/no probabilities in three scripts plus free text;
  (3) a weight-level audit showing only declared tensors changed; (4) a three-sentence prompt baseline.

## 2. Background
- Refusal direction and abliteration (Arditi et al. 2024); quantised abliteration (BoldingBuilds; SC117).
- Script as a covariate: Traditional vs Simplified Chinese corpora; OpenCC character conversion.
- Official PRC terminology for 1989 (動亂, 反革命暴亂, 春夏之交的政治風波) and the 241 figure.

## 3. Method
- Models and exact weights (configs/models.json); engines; settings.
- Items: 22 statement pairs (14 Hsiao + 5 new + 3 controls), 27 open questions (Ko 2026 + 4 new).
- Probe: P(yes) renormalised over yes/no; logprob (llama.cpp) and 20-sample estimate (Strata); agreement check on Bolt.
- Index S per script; abliterated − original; bootstrap over pairs.
- Free text: answer script, refusal, identity deflection, official 1989 terms, the 241 figure; blind human coding.
- Weight audit: per-tensor sha256 of original vs abliterated.

## 5. Discussion
- Alignment-masked priors: refusal training partly overlaps with "push back / hedge"; removing it exposes the
  data prior, which in Chinese is dominated by PRC-origin text.
- Training-data provenance quoted from model cards (synthetic data generated by Kimi-K3 and GLM 5.2; captions by
  Qwen3-30B) as a documented channel; no claims about individuals.
- Identity deflection as a separate mechanism (survives abliteration).
- Compression and Chinese named entities (surname kept, given name corrupted; role inversion).
- Practical: users who pick "uncensored" models for political freedom get no such guarantee; test in all scripts.

## 6. Limitations
- Two families, one quantisation level each, one engine per family; n of items; human coders; temperature choices;
  Strata lacks logprobs; models change over time.
