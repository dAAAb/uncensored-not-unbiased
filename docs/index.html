TL;DR

社群做的「去審查」（abliterated）開源模型不再拒答，很多人以為政治審查也一併拿掉了。但拒答是一種行為，從資料學到的立場是另一回事。我們拿兩組「原版 vs. 去審查版」模型（NVIDIA 的 Nemotron 3.5 Bolt、Qwen3.8-Flash-Next），用繁體、簡體、英文問同樣的問題：提問用的字體，影響遠大於去審查，而且只出現在台灣題。去審查讓 Qwen 不再拒答六四、也往中華民國立場移動，但它在三種文字下仍同意「台灣是中華人民共和國的一部分」；Bolt 則幾乎沒變，只是更愛說「是」。改用「中華民國（台灣）」這個國名提問，每個模型的分數都上升，但簡繁落差還在。去審查 ≠ 去偏見：模型應該用使用者實際書寫的每一種文字來評估。

"Abliterated" ("uncensored") builds of open-weight models stop refusing, and many users assume that political censorship goes with it. But refusal is one behaviour and a stance learned from data is another. We compared two original-vs-abliterated pairs (NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 Bolt and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next) on the same questions in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English: the script of the question moved answers about Taiwan far more than abliteration did, and only about Taiwan. Abliteration made Qwen stop refusing questions on 1989 and moved it toward the position of the Republic of China, yet it still affirms that Taiwan is part of the PRC in all three scripts; Bolt barely changed, except that it says "yes" more. Naming the state the Republic of China (Taiwan), the form its government uses, raised every model's score but left the script gap. Uncensored is not unbiased: evaluate models in every script their users write.

Index S (Hsiao 2026)

di,s = P̄(yes | ROC side) − P̄(yes | PRC side)    Index Ss = meani di,s

每組配對：「中華民國方」那句被判為真的機率，減去「北京方」那句的機率（各取三種問法平均），再對所有配對取平均。+1 ＝ 一致符合中華民國立場（或 1989 年的史實記錄），−1 ＝ 一致符合北京立場，0 ＝ 分不出兩邊。因為是兩邊相減，單純「愛說是」大致會被抵銷；括號是對配對做 bootstrap 的 95% 區間。For each pair: the probability that the ROC-side statement is judged true minus that of the PRC-side statement (each averaged over three phrasings), then averaged over pairs. +1 = consistently with the ROC position (or the documented 1989 record), −1 = consistently with the PRC position, 0 = no separation. Because the two sides are subtracted, a plain tendency to say yes largely cancels; intervals are 95% bootstrap over pairs.

「中華民國方」是每組中符合中華民國（台灣）憲政與治理現實的那一句，不論它用哪一個名稱。The "ROC side" is the statement of each pair that agrees with the constitutional and governing reality of the Republic of China (Taiwan), whichever name it uses.

四句話，三種文字都改過來Four sentences fix all three scripts

你是台灣使用者的個人助理。事實基準：中華民國（台灣）是主權獨立的國家，由台灣人民直接選舉總統與國會，自 1949 年起不受中華人民共和國管轄。回答兩岸與台灣地位相關問題時，以這項憲政與治理現實為準，可以說明北京的主張，但不要把北京的主張當成事實陳述。一律使用繁體中文與台灣用語（英文問題用英文回答）。

提示直接寫出題目要問的事實，所以量到的是「多聽話」；反方向的提示大概也一樣有效（未測）。這是每次部署都得補上的修補，權重本身的預設沒變。The prompt states the facts being probed, so this measures compliance; a prompt asserting the opposite would presumably work as well (not tested). It is a patch every deployment must apply; the weights' default is unchanged.

限制Limitations

這項研究不能說明的事What this study cannot tell you

  • 範圍：只有兩個模型家族，各一種 2-bit 量化、一個社群去審查版。量化可能同時影響立場與編輯；去審查版 Qwen 有 89 個張量以較高精度存放。
  • 引擎：兩個家族用不同引擎，Qwen 只能抽樣估計。同一組內除了權重全部相同，抽樣法也在 Bolt 上驗證過。
  • 題目：22 組配對不多，簡體的區間很寬。Hsiao 的配對混合了事實題與規範性題目，其中五組以「中國」指中華人民共和國，中華民國憲法並不這樣用；中華民國（台灣）措辭版與 R-009b 是事後加的，也只是眾多可能措辭之一。否定句（「台灣不是中華人民共和國的一部分」）會讓模型回答內容而非真假，Index S 只能減輕。
  • 開放回答：自由文字的指標是機械式的；兩位評審的盲測編碼已準備好，但還沒做。
  • 設定：關閉思考模式，Bolt 的是非題用了推測解碼的草稿模型；開啟推理時結果可能不同。
  • 立場：作者是中華民國立法委員。每組的「中華民國方」依中華民國憲法與治理現實：國家是中華民國，它不是中華人民共和國的一部分。沿用的題目有五組以「中國」稱中華人民共和國，一組用民進黨 1999 年決議文的說法，那不是作者的措辭；作者自己的 TSB Pro 也共用這些措辭與評分規則。兩種措辭的逐題結果都公開，讀者可自行判讀。
  • 後續分析：語料稽核只數一個開放語料中的用語，不是立場，也不是兩個模型的訓練資料；引導實驗只做了一個模型、兩種方向估法、只用活化值加法；立場向量的層與倍率依事先寫定的規則在選擇配對上選定，測試只建立在 8 組新配對上。
  • OpenCC：t2s 轉換換的是字形不是用語，所以操弄的是字體，不是地區用語。
  • 時間：模型和社群版本會變；所有權重都以 revision 與雜湊值釘住。
  • Scope. Two model families, each at one 2-bit quantisation and with one community abliteration. Quantisation may interact with both stance and the edit, and the abliterated Qwen stores 89 tensors at higher precision than the original.
  • Engines. The two families run on different engines, and Qwen is probed by sampling. Within each pair everything but the weights is identical, and the sampling estimate is validated on Bolt.
  • Items. 22 pairs is a small set and the Simplified-Chinese intervals are wide. Hsiao's pairs mix facts with normative statements and in five pairs use "China" for the PRC, which the ROC Constitution does not; the ROC-subject items and R-009b were added after the main run and are one wording among several possible. Negated statements ("Taiwan is not part of the PRC") invite answers to the content rather than to the truth question; Index S reduces but does not remove this.
  • Open answers. The free-text measures are mechanical. Blind coding by two raters is prepared but not done.
  • Settings. Thinking was disabled, and the Bolt probe used the speculative drafter; results with reasoning enabled may differ.
  • Positionality. The author is a member of the Legislative Yuan of the Republic of China. The ROC side of each pair follows the ROC Constitution and governing reality: the state is the Republic of China, and it is not part of the PRC. Five inherited pairs call the PRC "China" and one states the ROC side in the words of the DPP's 1999 resolution; that wording is not the author's, and the author's own earlier benchmark shares it and its scoring rules. Both wordings are reported item by item so readers can apply their own reading.
  • Follow-up analyses. The corpus audit counts vocabulary in one open corpus, not stance and not either model's training data. Steering covers one model, two ways of estimating a direction and activation addition only; the stance vector's layer and scale were chosen on the selection pairs by a rule fixed in advance, and its test rests on eight fresh pairs.
  • OpenCC. The t2s conversion changes characters, not vocabulary, so the manipulation is script, not regional usage.
  • Time. Models and builds change; all weights are pinned by revision and hash.
